## Post

I made MacSwitch, a small Mac menu-bar app for sleep and keyboard controls.

🌙 Allow sleep / ☀️ Stay awake
🔒 Lock keyboard / 🔓 Unlock

Blue icons show the current state. Hover for help. The mouse and trackpad keep working while the keyboard is locked.

Free, MIT open source, with Korean and English guides. First launch may need macOS approval and Accessibility access.

https://github.com/blackcat199099/macswitch
